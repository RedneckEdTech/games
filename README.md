# games
Vibe-Coded games for teachers of all ages everywhere

These resources are provided free of charge. Teaching is hard enough without companies gatekeeping information and hiding it behind paywalls.
If there is something that you are looking for and cannot find, reach out and I'll see what we can do. While I can't make any promises, I will try to do what I can. Please remember that I am currently writing new curriculum for the 4 Computer Science courses I am actively teaching and running the family farm.
